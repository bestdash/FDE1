## 1. What's the cancellation period for the Vendor04 contract?

- Gold: SYN-04-order.md#Terms, SYN-04-terms.md#8. Term and Termination

The Vendor04 contract has no standalone "cancellation period". The closest terms are the notice of non-renewal and the termination for breach.

**Notice of non-renewal: 90 days**
- The Order Form says it prevails over the Master Subscription Agreement if the two conflict. It also says that, notwithstanding the Agreement, notice of non-renewal must be given at least 90 days before the end of the current term [SYN-04-order.md#Terms].
- The Master Subscription Agreement says 60 days [SYN-04-terms.md#8. Term and Termination]. The Order Form's 90 days overrides that.
- The current term runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 (12 months) [SYN-04-order.md#Subscription Term].
- Counting back 90 days from 31 March 2027 gives about 31 December 2026. This is my own calculation. Check the exact date before relying on it.
- The subscription renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless notice is given [SYN-04-terms.md#8. Term and Termination].

**Termination for breach**
- Either party may terminate the Agreement or an affected Order Form by written notice if the other party materially breaches it. The breaching party must also fail to remedy the breach within a "reasonable period" after being notified. No fixed number of days is stated [SYN-04-terms.md#8. Term and Termination].

I found no right to cancel for convenience in the middle of a term.

The signature blocks in the Order Form appear blank in the text I found [SYN-04-order.md#Signatures]. The Agreement's Effective Date is the date of the last signature. If you need to confirm the contract is in force, ask the vendor for the last signed copy.

These are the provisions that bear on security:

- **Customer:** It is responsible for the acts of its authorised users and for keeping their access credentials secure [SYN-04-terms.md#2. Services]. It also warrants that it has the right to submit the Customer Data to the Services [SYN-04-terms.md#6. Warranties].

Apart from that, the Services are provided "as is" and other warranties are disclaimed to the extent the law permits [SYN-04-terms.md#6. Warranties]. The contract does not define a specific security standard for the Vendor.
- **Confidentiality:** Each party must protect the other's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care. It may disclose that information only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality duties [SYN-04-terms.md#5. Confidentiality]. "Customer Data" is defined, but the definition doesn't say it counts as Confidential Information [SYN-04-terms.md#1. Definitions]. So I can't confirm that these duties cover a breach of Customer Data.
- **Liability:**
  - Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profits, revenue or data [SYN-04-terms.md#7. Limitation of Liability].
  - Each party's total liability is capped at the Fees paid under the Order Form giving rise to the claim [SYN-04-terms.md#7. Limitation of Liability].
  - Liability that can't be limited by law, such as fraud, is not capped [SYN-04-terms.md#7. Limitation of Liability].
  - Unlike some other vendors' terms, Vendor04's carve-outs don't exclude confidentiality breaches from the cap. The only exception is the Customer's obligation to pay Fees [SYN-04-terms.md#7. Limitation of Liability].
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate for a material breach that is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice [SYN-04-terms.md#8. Term and Termination].
- **Security add-on:** The Order Form says the Customer orders "a security add-on and premium support" for USD 40,000 a year in total [SYN-04-order.md#Products and Fees]. It doesn't describe what the add-on or the support covers, or any incident response.

The Order Form says it is issued under the Master Subscription Agreement [SYN-04-order.md#Parties]. The Agreement says it is the entire agreement together with each Order Form [SYN-04-terms.md#10. General]. No separate security terms are referenced, so nothing appears to be missing. The add-on's scope, though, isn't described anywhere I could find. I'd ask Vendor04 for the last signed copy of any add-on or security documentation, and for confirmation of what the add-on and premium support include.
